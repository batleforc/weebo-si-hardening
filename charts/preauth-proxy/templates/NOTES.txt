*** The gateway is the authentication. ***
This process performs no authentication of its own: it hands every request that reaches it a
valid, full-privilege upstream credential. That is safe ONLY while a forward-auth gateway sits
ahead of it on the route. This chart renders no such gateway — point your IngressRoute (or
equivalent) at "{{ include "preauth-proxy.fullname" . }}" only from behind one, never directly.

Check readiness:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deployment/{{ include "preauth-proxy.fullname" . }}

Reading the logs — one structured line per acquisition and per renewal is the only signal
injection is working, since there are no metrics yet:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deploy/{{ include "preauth-proxy.fullname" . }} -f
